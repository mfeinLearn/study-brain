Calculus notes: derivatives and the chain rule (SAMPLE NOTES)

Partial derivative: treat every other variable as a constant.
d/dy of x^2 * y = x^2, because x^2 is a constant when we vary y.

Chain rule: for f(g(x)), the derivative is f'(g(x)) * g'(x).
Outer derivative evaluated at the inner function, times the inner derivative.
Backpropagation is the chain rule applied layer by layer.
